# Refund Policy — EU Addendum (approved)

### Premium refunds (EU)

For EU-region customers, premium-tier automatic refunds are capped at
**€40** regardless of risk score, pending VAT reconciliation work.

> Note: this addendum intentionally conflicts with the global PRD's $100
> premium limit; regional reconciliation is unresolved.
