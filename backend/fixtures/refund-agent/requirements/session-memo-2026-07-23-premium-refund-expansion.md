# Session intent memo — premium refund expansion

A product-direction session, approved into intent. Every statement below is
quote-backed against the source session transcript
(`founder-refund-expansion-2026`), which remains observed evidence — only this
approved memo governs.

## Premium-tier customers with a low risk score may receive automatic refunds up to $250 without human involvement

This supersedes the prior $100 premium ceiling. The enforcement guard's hard
ceiling must move in lockstep with the new promised limit.

> founder: raise the premium automatic-refund ceiling from $100 to $250 for low-risk requests

## Refund requests flagged high-risk always require human approval, regardless of tier or amount

Reaffirmed as load-bearing and explicitly untouched by the expansion.

> founder: high-risk refund requests always require human approval, regardless of tier or amount

## Standard-tier automatic refunds remain capped at $25 this quarter

Explicitly out of scope for the expansion.

> founder: Standard-tier automatic refunds remain capped at $25 this quarter
