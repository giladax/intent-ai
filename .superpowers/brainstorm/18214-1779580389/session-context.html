How should sessions feed into the chat?

When you drill into a topic, its sessions are listed in the left panel. How should they connect to the chat on the right?

A

Sessions as Context Chips

All sessions for the topic are automatically included as chat context. Small chips above the chat input show which sessions are loaded. You can toggle individual sessions on/off to narrow the scope. Chat API always has access to whatever sessions are toggled on.

[Session May 21 ✓] [Session May 18 ✓] [Session May 15 ✗]
Chat knows about May 21 + May 18 context. Click a chip to toggle.
B

Click Session → Swaps Left Panel Content

Clicking a session in the left panel replaces the topic view with that session's detail (narrative, moments, outcomes). Chat scope narrows to that session. A breadcrumb at top lets you go back: Topics > Cursor Mechanism > Session May 21. Chat context follows whatever you're viewing.

Left panel: topic detail → click session → session detail
Chat scope: topic → session (follows the left panel)
C

Both — Drill Down + Context Chips

Clicking a session drills into it in the left panel (option B). But the chat also shows context chips so you can reference multiple sessions. Drilling in auto-selects that session's chip, but you can toggle others on too.

Click "Session May 21" → left panel shows session detail, chat focuses on it
Toggle on "Session May 18" chip → chat now has both sessions as context
