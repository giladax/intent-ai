💬 Moment Detection
Why was regex removed from classification?
Regex patterns like /\b(because|actually)\b/ were used to classify developer engagement. In session 3 (May 23), the developer ran a chromosome evaluation comparing regex-based pre-computation (3c) vs exchange pairs only (3b). Regex-contaminated 3c scored 4.35/5 — worse than 3b at 4.80/5. After replacing with Haiku structured output, 3c jumped to 4.80/5.
